⚠️ Notification Issues?

ℹ️

System restrictions are the #1 cause. Your phone puts apps to sleep to save battery. You must whitelist LerriAI manually.

🤖 Android (Samsung, Pixel)
  • Go to: Settings > Apps > LerriAI > Battery
  • Select: "Unrestricted" (or "No optimization").
  • Go to: Mobile Data and enable "Allow background data usage".
🟠 Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO

MIUI is very aggressive. You MUST do this:

  • Go to: Settings > Apps > Manage Apps > LerriAI
  • Enable: "Autostart" (Crucial!).
  • Battery Saver: Select "No restrictions".
🍎 Apple iOS (iPhone)
  • Step 1: Tap the "Share" button and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • Step 2: Open the app from the Home Screen icon.
  • Step 3: Go to Settings inside the app and enable notifications again.
  • (Notifications do not work inside Safari browser)
🔍 Final Checks

Ensure "Do Not Disturb" or "Focus Mode" is OFF.
